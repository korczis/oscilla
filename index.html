Digital representability

The Web Audio engine can mathematically generate a requested signal within digital limits, but the physical speaker/headphones may attenuate, distort, or fail to reproduce it. OSCILLA cannot detect actual acoustic output.

On iPhone and iPad the ring/silent switch can mute browser audio.

About OSCILLA

OSCILLA is an interactive sound and frequency lab: an educational instrument for generating signals with the Web Audio API and seeing them as waveform, spectrum, frequency motion, signal path, harmonics and interference.

Safety. Audio signals can be uncomfortable or harmful at excessive output levels. Start low, especially with headphones. Perceived loudness is not a reliable measure of acoustic output, particularly at very low or very high frequencies. Digital signal generation does not guarantee that your playback hardware reproduces the requested frequency accurately.

What it is not. Not a hearing test, not a medical or therapeutic device, not a calibrated measurement instrument. Levels are relative; no SPL is shown or implied.

Privacy. Everything runs in this page. Presets and theme stay in localStorage, history in sessionStorage; the optional microphone is analysed locally and never recorded or uploaded.

Keyboard: Space plays (hold) or triggers programmed patterns when focus is not in a control; Escape stops immediately; the frequency map accepts arrows, Shift, Alt and PageUp/PageDown when focused.
