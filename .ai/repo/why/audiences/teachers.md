# Teachers demonstrating sound in a lesson or lecture

## Who this is

Somebody showing beating between 440 Hz and 442 Hz (`hear-440-and-442-hz-beating`), the range
of hearing with a 20 Hz-20 kHz sweep (`sweep-20-hz-to-20-khz`) or a filter's effect, to a
class, and who has set up the demonstration the day before (`round-trip-a-configuration`).

## How they work

On a lecture-room computer and its speakers, sometimes without a network or the right to
install anything, with the audience listening. The demonstration has to start when they press
play and stop when they press stop.

## What goes wrong

A tone that starts with a click or comes out much louder than expected through the room
speakers. A sound that keeps playing after Stop. A page that needs a network the room does not
have. A claim in the demonstration that turns out to have no source.
